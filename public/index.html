Privacy & Data Governance

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Transparency Notice

📍 1. Device Geolocation & Coordinates

When you choose "Use my location", coordinates (latitude & longitude) are accessed strictly inside your browser via navigator.geolocation. Coordinates are used solely to query atmospheric modeling for your vicinity. Your location is never stored on remote servers, never recorded in server databases, and never transmitted to advertising networks.

💾 2. Local Device Storage (No Cookies)

Mausam India uses zero tracking cookies, session identifiers, or marketing pixels. The browser's local storage (localStorage) is used strictly on your device to persist your preferences:

  • Measurement Units: Celsius (°C) vs. Fahrenheit (°F)
  • Interface Theme: Automatic sunrise/sunset, Light, or Dark mode
  • Saved Favorites: List of cities you manually pin for quick access

📡 3. Upstream Atmospheric Telemetry

Weather, air quality, and imagery requests are fetched through serverless Cloudflare edge caching to protect upstream API reliability. The independent data sources are:

  • Open-Meteo: Numerical atmospheric modeling and CPCB AQI estimates (CC BY 4.0).
  • OpenAQ: Physical ground CAAQMS monitor telemetry via server-side edge proxy.
  • NASA GIBS: True-color MODIS/VIIRS satellite cloud imagery.
  • RainViewer & Esri: Animated Doppler precipitation radar and basemap tiles.
